Paw Peak

A climbing course in the sky. Swing and push your way up the steps to the golden trophy.

  • Push off blocks with your hands to move, like Gorilla Tag
  • Swing your arms down hard to launch yourself upward
  • Right stick turns
  • You play as your own player model
  • Collect all 12 coins on the way up
  • At the top, find the key card in the hamster maze, dodge the monster and escape through the exit

Open this page in your headset’s browser to play.
